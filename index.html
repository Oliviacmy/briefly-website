Hong Kong News Monday · 6 July 2026

Police seize HK$128 million of suspected cocaine in Yuen Long warehouse

Hong Kong police arrested three men after officers found about 172kg of suspected cocaine inside metal containers at a Yuen Long warehouse, with the suspected drugs estimated to be worth about HK$128 million. Police said the suspects were caught while attempting to cut open the containers. The case points to the continuing use of industrial and logistics spaces in serious drug-trafficking operations, and underlines why enforcement agencies pay close attention to cargo movement, storage sites and cross-border supply routes.

Source: RTHK

香港警方在元朗一個倉庫拘捕三名男子,並在金屬容器內檢獲約172公斤懷疑可卡因,估計市值約1.28億港元,是近日較大型的毒品檢獲案件之一。警方表示,疑犯當時正試圖切開相關容器。案件反映嚴重販毒活動仍可能利用工業及物流空間作為藏毒或轉運地點,亦說明執法部門為何需要密切留意貨物流動、倉儲地點及跨境供應路線。

Why it matters

Large drug seizures are not only crime stories; they are also logistics, border-control and public-safety stories. The scale suggests organised trafficking networks continue to test Hong Kong’s role as a regional transport and storage hub.

Global News Monday · 6 July 2026

Iran mourns late supreme leader as succession questions loom

Iran held mass funeral prayers for its late supreme leader, with thousands of mourners gathering in Tehran, while Mojtaba Khamenei — widely watched in succession discussions — did not make a public appearance. The funeral is a symbolic moment, but the larger issue is political continuity: leadership transitions in Iran can influence domestic power balances, regional security, oil-market expectations and relations with Washington and Israel.

Source: Anadolu Ajansı · RTHK

伊朗為已故最高領袖舉行大型葬禮祈禱,數以千計民眾在德黑蘭聚集悼念;在接班安排中備受關注的穆吉塔巴哈梅內伊,則未有在葬禮公開露面。葬禮本身具有象徵意義,但更大的問題是政治延續:伊朗領導層交替可能影響國內權力平衡、地區安全、油價預期,以及與美國和以色列的關係。

Why it matters

Iran is central to Middle East security. Any uncertainty around succession can affect diplomatic calculations, sanctions policy, energy markets and conflict risk — and for Hong Kong readers, that impact is likely to appear first through oil prices and markets.
